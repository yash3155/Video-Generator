### Core Concept & Governing Principle

In Chapter 4 of *A Complete Guide to Volume Price Analysis*, Anna Coulling defines the core purpose of **Volume Price Analysis (VPA)** as identifying one of two states: **Validation (Confirmation)** or an **Anomaly** [1, 2]. 

This single-candle analysis is governed by **Wyckoff’s Third Law: Effort vs. Result** [3-5]:
* **Effort** is represented by **Trading Volume** (the activity bar) [3-5].
* **Result** is represented by **Price Spread** (the distance between the open and close on the candlestick) [3-5].

---

### Part 1: Single-Candle Confirmations (Validations)

When volume and price spread are in agreement, the move is validated and indicates genuine market activity supported by major operators [1, 3].

#### 1. Wide Spread Candle + High Volume
* **Setup & Definition**: A wide-bodied candlestick (strong price movement up or down with small wicks) accompanied by well above-average volume [3, 6].
* **Logical Formula**: \\(\text{High Effort (Volume)} = \text{High Result (Wide Spread)}\\) [3].
* **Key Rules & Interpretation**: Volume validates price [3, 6]. This confirms that the price move is genuine and backed by insiders/market makers [3]. Market sentiment is firmly bullish (for an up candle) or bearish (for a down candle) [3, 6].
* **Trader Action**: Maintain and hold existing positions in the direction of the move until an anomaly appears [3].

#### 2. Narrow Spread Candle + Low Volume
* **Setup & Definition**: A narrow-bodied candlestick (small price move with small wicks) accompanied by well below-average volume [4].
* **Logical Formula**: \\(\text{Low Effort (Volume)} = \text{Low Result (Narrow Spread)}\\) [4].
* **Key Rules & Interpretation**: Volume validates price [4]. A small price change requires only a small amount of effort [4]. This represents a true, unmanipulated picture of low market activity [4].
* **Trader Action**: Treat as normal market behavior requiring no immediate defensive action [4].

---

### Part 2: Single-Candle Anomalies

Anomalies occur when volume and price spread disagree, serving as the first warning signal of market manipulation or an impending trend change [1, 2, 5].

#### 3. Wide Spread Candle + Low Volume (The Trap Move)
* **Setup & Definition**: A wide-bodied candlestick accompanied by below-average or low volume [5, 7].
* **Logical Formula**: \\(\text{Low Effort (Volume)} \neq \text{High Result (Wide Spread)} \longrightarrow \text{Anomaly}\\) [5].
* **Key Rules & Interpretation**: Indicates a **fake move or trap** set by market makers [7, 8]. If the price move were genuine, buyers/insiders would support it with high volume [7, 9]. Instead, price is being marked up (or down) without genuine buying or selling pressure [7, 9].
* **Market Insider Mechanism**: Market makers mark prices higher to "feel out" market sentiment or trap retail traders into weak positions without committing their own capital [7-9].
* **Real-World Case Studies & Examples**:
  * **Equity Market Opening Bell (1-Minute Chart)**: Market makers push the stock price higher at the open to test buying interest. If low volume shows retail buyers aren't participating, market makers quickly mark the price back down [7, 9].
  * **Forex News Release (Stop Hunting)**: On fundamental news releases, price jumps violently on low volume. Insiders use the news spike to suck in retail traders and trigger stop-loss orders before reversing the market [8].
* **Trader Action**: If holding a position in the direction of the spread, question it immediately and prepare to exit; if not in a trade, stay out [7, 9, 10].

#### 4. Narrow Spread Candle + High Volume (Absorption & Market Weakness/Strength)
* **Setup & Definition**: A narrow-bodied candlestick accompanied by high or ultra-high volume [9, 11].
* **Logical Formula**: \\(\text{High Effort (Volume)} \neq \text{Low Result (Narrow Spread)} \longrightarrow \text{Anomaly}\\) [11].
* **Key Rules & Interpretation**: Represents a massive amount of effort generating minimal price progression [11]. Signals market weakness at trend tops (overbought) or market strength/stopping power at trend bottoms (oversold) [11-13].
* **Market Insider Mechanism (Absorption)**:
  * **At Market Tops**: High volume with narrow spread shows an intense battle where retail buyers are trying to push prices higher, but every wave of buying is met with insiders selling inventory to take profits [12, 14-16]. Insider supply absorbs retail demand, capping price progress [12, 14, 16].
  * **At Market Bottoms**: High volume on a narrow spread indicates insider buying is absorbing retail panic selling, preventing prices from dropping further [12, 13, 17].
* **Author Analogies**:
  * **The Supertanker & Sponge/Mopping-Up Analogy**: The market has momentum like a supertanker and cannot stop or turn on a dime [18]. When mopping up a spill with paper towels, the first pass absorbs most of the liquid, but a second pass is needed to complete the job; similarly, insiders absorb retail pressure over multiple candles/passes during consolidation [18].
  * **Warehouse Clearance Analogy**: Market makers operate like wholesale merchants clearing out inventory from their warehouse at retail prices near market tops before pushing prices down to restock at wholesale levels [12, 16, 19].
* **Trader Action**: Treat as an early warning signal of market weakness or absorption; take profits off the table or prepare for a potential trend reversal [16, 20].
