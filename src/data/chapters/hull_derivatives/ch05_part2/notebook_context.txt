Valuing Forward Contracts Mid-Life\n\n* **Definition & Core Concept**: At the inception of a forward contract (\\\\(t = 0\\\\)), the delivery price \\\\(K\\\\) is set equal to the current forward price \\\\(F_0\\\\), making the initial value of the contract \\\\(f = 0\\\\) [1-3].
